Toronto-based Strip Lacquer are comfortable existing in unease.
Drawing from art rock experimentation, industrial abrasiveness and post-punk ethos, they encompass sharp emotional songwriting with nuanced dissonance and a diverse sonic palette.
Laced in a delicate yet murky disposition, shifting seamlessly from amoral to vulnerable, fixated by a desire to dissect the grotesque.
